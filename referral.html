INDEPENDENT REFERRAL & REVENUE-SHARE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on this by and between:

THE D-TECH ECOSYSTEM (Pty) Ltd (trading as D-TECH or D-TECH Services), a private company registered in South Africa (hereinafter referred to as the "Company" or "Licensor"),

and

, ID Number: (hereinafter referred to as the "Referral Partner" or "Marketer").

1. APPOINTMENT & ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Company hereby appoints the Referral Partner on a non-exclusive, independent contractor basis to market, introduce, and refer prospective paying clients to the Company’s proprietary software platforms:

  • D-TECH RES (Residence Management System); and
  • D-TECH Business Portal (Business CRM & Invoicing Platform).

1.2 Independent Status: Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship. The Referral Partner has no authority to bind the Company to any contract or legal liability.

2. REVENUE-SHARE & COMMISSION STRUCTURE

2.1 Commission Rates: For each prospective client introduced by the Referral Partner who successfully subscribes to and pays for an active monthly software subscription:

  • D-TECH Business Portal: The Referral Partner shall earn 50% (fifty percent) of the net monthly subscription fees received by the Company from that referred client.
  • D-TECH RES (Residence Management): The Referral Partner shall earn 40% (forty percent) of the net monthly subscription fees received by the Company from that referred client.

2.2 Duration of Revenue Share: The commission shall apply to each qualifying client for a maximum initial period of twelve (12) consecutive billing cycles (1 year) from the date of the client’s first cleared subscription payment.

2.3 Year-Two Renegotiation: Prior to the conclusion of the initial twelve (12) month period, both parties shall negotiate in good faith regarding an ongoing account maintenance rate or renewed percentage based on business metrics and portfolio performance.

2.4 Payment Condition (Cash Inflow): Commission is strictly earned upon the actual receipt and clearance of subscription funds into the Company's business bank account. No commission is payable on free trials, pilots, non-payments, chargebacks, or refunds.

2.5 Disbursement: The Company agrees to disburse all accrued monthly commission payments to the Referral Partner's designated South African bank account within 5 (five) business days following the end of each billing calendar month.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & PROPRIETARY RIGHTS

3.1 The Referral Partner expressly acknowledges that all software, source code, UI designs, brand marks, 3D/2D engines, documentation, databases, and trade secrets related to D-TECH, D-TECH RES, and D-TECH Business Portal remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Company.

3.2 The Referral Partner is granted a limited, revocable, non-exclusive license to use the Company’s approved marketing collateral, brochures, and platform demo links solely for introducing potential clients.

4. CLIENT OWNERSHIP & ACCOUNT ONBOARDING

4.1 All clients, student residences, and registered businesses remain the sole operational and contractual customers of the Company.

4.2 The Referral Partner's role is strictly limited to lead generation and introductory communication. The Company remains entirely responsible for technical onboarding, software uptime, bug fixes, and hosting.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-CIRCUMVENTION

5.1 Both parties agree to keep strictly confidential any non-public business information, source code details, pricing strategies, customer lists, and financial figures disclosed during this collaboration.

5.2 Non-Circumvention: The Referral Partner agrees not to bypass, copy, or replicate the Company’s platform architecture or attempt to direct referred clients to competing platforms.

6. TERM & TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement takes effect upon signing and continues on a month-to-month basis or until terminated.

6.2 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days written notice (including via confirmed email or WhatsApp communication) to the other party.

6.3 Vesting of Earned Commissions: In the event of standard termination, any active paying clients already successfully introduced by the Referral Partner prior to the notice date shall continue to earn their respective 40% or 50% revenue share for the remainder of their original 12-month billing period, provided the client remains active and paying.

6.4 Immediate Termination: The Company reserves the right to terminate this Agreement immediately if the Referral Partner engages in fraudulent activity, misrepresents platform features, or disparages the brand.

7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of South Africa. Any disputes arising shall fall under the jurisdiction of the courts of South Africa.
